## Overview

The MATE ROV Competition is an international underwater robotics competition for high school
and university teams: design and build a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) to complete simulated
underwater tasks, then present the design to judges through a technical paper and poster.

As an upperclassman and robotics team officer, I co-led a team of students as **Mechanical Team
Lead**, with a holistic view across the whole vehicle while focusing personally on CAD,
fabrication, and assembly.

## My Role & Contributions

- Co-led team training in CAD, fabrication, soldering, and Arduino for less experienced members
  before the season began
- Divided the vehicle into modules (frame, thrusters, sensors, claw/arms, mounts) across small
  sub-teams, while personally contributing across all of them
- Directly designed and built four range-of-motion arms, the CAD for the frame, and the
  electronics mount
- Participated in an internal R&D group prototyping experimental concepts for future seasons

## Design Approach

We settled on an eight-thruster frame for maneuverability through redundancy, built around
aluminum T-slot extrusion with 3D-printed frame connectors and thruster guards, a laser-cut/3D
printed claw, and a Blue Robotics waterproof electronics enclosure.

Design priorities were performance, cost efficiency, and safety/sustainability — which meant real
trade-offs. Our in-house 3-axis R&D arm wasn't ready to competition standard, so we simplified to
a lighter claw design. We also evaluated cheaper waterproof enclosures, but testing showed they
leaked under pressure, so we kept the more expensive, reliable Blue Robotics enclosure rather than
compromise on watertightness.

## Results

- **Placed 8th of 29 teams** at the regional competition
- Delivered a fully functional ROV meeting nearly all competition criteria
- Helped train 15+ students in robotics fundamentals over the season
- Directly experienced the tradeoffs of leading a technical team under a fixed competition
  deadline — balancing ambition against reliability
