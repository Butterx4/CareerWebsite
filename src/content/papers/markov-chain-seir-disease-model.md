Built and analyzed increasingly detailed Markov chain models (SEIR, then SEIR with an absorbing
immune state, then a full SEIR-VIm model including vaccination) to study how initial conditions
and model structure affect long-term disease-state distributions in a population, including
formal eigenvalue-based derivation of stationary distributions.
