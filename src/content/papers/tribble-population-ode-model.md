Used a modified logistic ODE with a saturating harvesting term to compare long-term population
outcomes across species with different crowding coefficients, identifying bistable regimes and
basins of attraction, then extended the model to time-varying (seasonal) harvesting to study
limit-cycle behavior in a non-autonomous system.
