Cross-disciplinary research with Biomedical Engineering and Computer Science collaborators,
building a simulation framework to help clinicians compare interventional strategies for Fontan
patients at risk of pulmonary arteriovenous malformations. My contribution centered on the
computational/modeling pipeline: processing segmented vessel geometry and simulating
hemodynamic interventions in MATLAB.
